# Round 789, lane X: the Akatsuki test content (a custom avatar set in a `.pk3`)

What was asked: prepare the content for the round's end-to-end test of asset packs and avatars.
An Akatsuki robe for the four bodies and an Akatsuki straw hat, each a new item with its own id,
name and icon, made by retexturing retail avatar meshes (no new meshes), shipped in
`z_akatsuki.pk3`. The content is fan content made for this test and lives outside the repo; only
the tools are committed. The pack layer (lanes K, G) and the avatar slots (the avatar lanes) are
built in parallel, so the final test is left for after their merge (section 6).

Labels: **READ** (earlier rounds' binary reads, cited), **MEASURED** (the operator's archives in
`/opt/sro-files/client/` on the render box, Godot 4.7.2 on the box, no native library in
`/opt/opensro-client-789x`), **INFERRED**, **OURS**, **OPEN**.

## 2. What a new item needs

ITEM_FORMAT.md, "What a new item needs", has the table. In short:

- **The row (READ round 741 for the index rule):** a part named by `textdata\itemdata.txt`. A
  pack cannot append to a file, it replaces it (docs/TODO.md: a file in a pack uses its pk2 path;
  replacing and adding are the same step), so the pack carries `itemdata.txt` = the retail 63 names
  plus `ItemData_78900.txt`, and that part. Each row is the base row copied whole with columns 1
  (id), 2 (code), 3 (internal name), 5/6 (name/description string ids), 52 (worn model), 54
  (icon) changed. Every other column (type 3/1/13/x, Country 3, ReqGender, cash flag 1, prices,
  durability, the drop model) is the base's (MEASURED: the row dump of 25809/24271/25799/25800).
- **The name (MEASURED round 731):** the client reads the text tables `textdataname.txt` lists.
  The pack carries that list plus `TextData_Akatsuki789.txt` (8 rows in the retail row shape).
  Our client read the two retail tables by fixed name; `ItemTableReader.object_name_paths` now
  reads the list (OURS change, retail's rule; the fixed pair is the fallback).
- **The icon:** a new `.ddj` under `icon\` in the base icons' format.
- **The model chain:** item row -> new `.bsr` -> new `.bmt` -> new `.ddj`; the `.bms` stay retail.
  The row cannot point at a new `.bsr` alone: the texture is named by the `.bmt`, and the `.bmt`
  by the `.bsr` (READ, BSR/BMT formats).
- **charactervisualchange.txt:** nothing. The avatar rows (ids 200-213, ConditionID 2) key on the
  packed type word (helm 3756/3757, dress 5804/5805, attachments 7852/7853, 9900), not the item
  (MEASURED); a new 3/1/13 item falls under them (INFERRED: the arbitration `FUN_00a5da10` is not
  read).
- **The server:** the items CSV (`cmd/export-refdata -mode items` over the index's parts, the
  pack's part last). Run on the box: 15,342 rows, `78901..78904` among them as 3/1/13/2 and 3/1/13/1
  with their sexes (`/var/tmp/x789/items_with_akatsuki.csv`). No shop or package table is needed
  for `/give`. For the Test Items NPC's avatar tab (an avatar lane's), a rule like
  `{"code": "^ITEM_MALL_AVATAR_[MW]_OPENSRO_AKATSUKI(_HAT)?$", "type": "3/1/13/*"}` lists them.

## 3. The tools (committed, synthetic tests only)

|---|---|

| `server/internal/retexture`, `server/cmd/retexture` | copy a `.bsr`/`.bmt` (raw or zlib) with length-prefixed strings renamed (exact match, case and `/` `\` ignored), every other byte kept, the `.bsr` table shifted; refuses a bare/full texture form change and a missing string. `-list` prints the strings |
| `client/tools/avatar_retexture_probe.gd` | `--chain` (the row-to-texture chain with the layer that served each file), `--items/--outfits --out` (front and back on both races), `--dump` (mesh JSON), `--overlay=<dir>` (an unpacked pack served before the archives; the probe's own stand-in for the pack layer, OURS) |
| `ItemTableReader.object_name_paths` | the name tables from `textdataname.txt` (test: `item_table_parts_test.gd`) |

**How the textures were painted (OURS).** Every texel gets the bind-pose point and normal it maps
to (the mesh dumps rasterized in UV space). The robes' UVs are mirrored left and right: 95 % (M) /
86 % (F) of texels are used by two places, 9 % / 2 % once `|x|` is taken (MEASURED), so clouds are
placed on the `x >= 0` half and come out in mirrored pairs (a cloud on the spine is symmetric).
The base is near-black (#161619) times the base texture's blurred luminance (clamped 0.85-1.15),
so the folds stay. The clouds are planar decals in 3D: a union-of-circles shape, red (#B0181E)
with a white rim and curls, on back, front, hem and both sleeves. Texels whose normal faces the
body axis on the collar and the hem are the lining, red. The knight's alpha is its env-map mask
(95 % under 128 on a `0x341` layer), kept 0 where 0 and capped at 34 elsewhere so the black is
not chrome; the fur has no alpha. The hat's islands are classed by their 3D extent: straw cone,
brim and underside (a procedural weave in the hat's own angle and slant), rim tags (darker straw),
the cord (rope), the hanging cloths (pale, #D4CFBE) and the feather (alpha 0, cut by the layer's
alpha test). The icons are front renders of the painted items in the base icon's frame.

**Rebuild:** decode the bases (`ddjtool decode`), dump the meshes (`avatar_retexture_probe.gd
--items=25809,24271,9340,9341 --dump=...`), paint (`python3 paint_akatsuki.py <png dir> <dump
dir> <out>`; needs numpy and Pillow, which the box lacks, so it ran on the dev machine), copy the
PNGs to `/var/tmp/x789/paint/`, then `/var/tmp/x789/src/build_pack.sh` (rows, `retexture` x8,
`ddjtool encode -like` x7, zip, manifest) and `export_items.sh` for the server CSV.

## 7. Second pass (the coordinator's review)

Asked: the hat's hanging cloth should be thin paper streamers with gaps and an open back, not a
veil; no cloud may be cut by a UV seam; leave the default-wear show-through alone.

- **The base materials' alpha mode (READ through `ObjectLoader.layer_draw_rule`, printed by
  `avatar_retexture_probe.gd --chain` since this pass):** the satgat layer is flags `0x341`, no
  env-map or MTRL mod: alpha test, reference 128, GREATEREQUAL, no blend, two-sided. The fur is
  the same. The knight layer has an env-map mod (header flags 3), so its test reference is 1: its
  alpha is the reflection mask, as section 4 inferred. So the hat is painted as a cut-out (alpha
  0 or 255), never a blend.
- **The hanging cloth (MEASURED):** the satgat has no cloth at the front. One panel hangs behind
  the head (angles 55..132 degrees, used once); the side panels (around 0 and 180 degrees) are
  shared by both sides and by three stacked layers. Each panel's height is linear in its UVs (fit
  R2 0.969 side, 0.999 back), so the strips are laid out in UV space, across the height gradient:
  zig-zag paper strips 0.15 wide every 0.34, of varying length (OURS). The back panel keeps only
  its two outer strips, so the hair shows. 1,711 of 7,792 cloth texels stay (22 %).
- **Whole clouds (OURS, measured per cloud):** each target is moved (up to 1.4 in x and y) and
  shrunk (to 60 %) until (a) a render of the outermost surface in the cloud's own plane puts every
  point of the cloud plus a margin of 0.12 on surface, all on one texel island; (b) its texels
  touch no other cloud's; (c) its texels are not shared by another place in part only (all or
  none; the sleeves' front and back share all); (d) for the lower coat, its texels plus a 0.7
  margin show nowhere in a render from the other side (the back of the coat shows through the
  open front, both layers two-sided); (e) on the women's robe, a mirrored pair keeps 0.9 between
  its copies (the front panels close over each other). A target that fits nowhere is dropped.
  The painter paints a cloud only on its own island. Placed (each shows as a mirrored pair):
  men 6 (sleeve front, sleeve back, chest, back upper, back middle, front middle; the hem targets
  were removed after the stand pose showed the back hem through the front opening), women 5
  (sleeve front, sleeve back, front hem, back middle, back hem). The spine clouds and the women's front-middle pair
  find no whole placement and are dropped.
- **Renders** (stand pose, China and Europe body, front and back), `/var/tmp/x789/shots2/`:
  `78901_78903_item_mall_avatar_m_opensro_akatsuki.png`,
  `78902_78904_item_mall_avatar_w_opensro_akatsuki.png`, the four single items, `icons_sheet.png`,

  cascade is by file, not by line). A merge rule for list files would be OURS; not decided.
