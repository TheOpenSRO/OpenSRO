## What retail does (READ, both binaries)

Two read-only subagents read the binaries on the box. Their tables were then filed as follows:

- `docs/protocol/pc_spawn.md`: the PC spawn record, field by field, both sides;
- `docs/protocol/opcodes/movement.md`: 0xB021 in full, the 0x7021 gates, and two corrections.

The rules the server now follows:

- **The record.**
  - The server writes it with `CGObjPC::vslot_294` `0x4e5a10` in mode 2, then `vslot_293` `0x4e5b00`,
    `0x4a9b70` and `0x48b630`.
  - The client reads it with `FUN_008a6bd0` and CICUser slot 25 (`0x9de2f0`), whose readers are
    slot 22 `0x9dbc30` and slot 23 `0x9ddc40` -> `0x9d1300`.
  - It is 32 fields. Every field the server writes, the client reads in the same order and width.
  - Four implicit contracts:
    - a storage size is never 0, because the client always reads the count;
    - the avatar list's plus test;
    - the job state comes from the slot-8 suit, which also decides the guild block;
    - the buff token's `+0x61`.
- **The group.**
  - `0x3017 {u8 kind, u16 count}`, then `0x3019` frames, then an empty `0x3018`. There is one group
    per block, sent only when the count is not 0.
  - **A data frame carries 4,052 bytes**: the message buffer's 0x1000 (`0x471500` / `0x471580`), less
    the 6-byte header, less the `0x2209 {u32, u16}` envelope to a player, less the 0x20 that
    `FUN_00430a20` holds back.
  - The client's `0x3017` asserts if a group is already open (`0x8abfa0`). Groups never interleave.
  - The despawn group carries u32 ids (`FUN_00533b90`).
- **Who gets what.** A player is put in a block (`CMsgBlock` slot 2, `0x534340`) or taken out of one
  (slot 3, `0x5344b0`):
  - The block and its 8 neighbours send it their objects as group spawns (or group despawns).
  - Their players get its 0x3015 (or 0x3016).
  - **A walk across a block line sends only the 3 x 3 difference.** The map at block `+0x1c`
    (`0x534610` / `0x534680`) holds `3x3(A) - 3x3(K)` for each neighbour K. Despawns go out first,
    then spawns. Blocks still in view get nothing.
  - A non-adjacent jump falls back to the full remove and insert.
- **A move**, 0xB021, goes to every PC in the mover's 3 x 3, the mover included
  (`CMsgBlockObject::FUN_00534150` -> `0x5342a0`).
- **0x3015's spawn type** for a walk's crossing is 4 (`0x485b36`). That the stack slot is that
  argument is INFERRED.

## What OpenSRO does now

### C1: the interest area (`server/internal/world`, `server/internal/session/playerview.go`)

**The hub.**

- `world.Hub` files every player under its block (`Entity.Cell`, `combat.CellOf`, 320 units).
- `Relocate` moves a player and returns a `MoveResult`:
  - the move's recipients: the sessions of the 3 x 3 window of the new block;
  - and, when it crossed a line, `Gained` (in the new window, not the old) and `Lost` (the reverse).
- `Enter`, `Leave` and `ChangeZone` return the window, not the zone.
- `ViewOfCharacter` answers "who sees X".

**The messages, all READ forms.**

- **World entry** (`enterOwned`):
  - The result's `Others` list is empty.
  - After the result, the arriver is sent the players in its window as **group spawns, one group per
    block**, each player's record followed by its buffs.
  - Those players are sent the arriver's **0x3015**.
- **Zone change** (`warpTo`): the window it left gets **0x3016**. That includes a window it lands in
  view of again (retail's remove and insert). The new window is then handled as a world entry.
- **A move across a block line** (`handleMove`, and a skill's jump in `moveBySkill`, through
  `afterRelocate` -> `crossPlayer`):
  - the mover gets the group despawns of the lost and the group spawns of the gained;
  - those players get its 0x3016 / 0x3015.
- **A leave**: the window gets 0x3016.
- **A move** goes to the window's players that hold the mover. It is still OpenSRO's 0x0024 (C3).

**The record** (`server/internal/pcspawn`, filled by `session/pcrecord.go`):

- The model and the scale byte.
- The worn items of slots 0..8 with their effective plus.
- The unique id: the character id's low 32 bits, OpenSRO's rule since round 710.
- The region-local position in the zone's frame.
- The wire angle.
- The life state.
- **The motion (4 while sitting) and the body mode (1 while berserk).** These replace the result's
  Seated list and the 0x30BF replay that used to follow an arrival.
- The run speed with buffs.
- The name.
- The job state from the slot-8 suit, through the item table (`SetSpawnItemRefs`).

**The known set.**

- Every session that went through the world entry has a `playerView`: the characters its client holds
  a body for.
- Every message *about* a player goes through the recipient's view lock: its spawn, despawn, move,
  motion (0x30BF kind 1), body mode (kind 4), equipment (0x3038 / 0x3039), buffs, and its own casts'
  `OpSkillCast`.
  - A spawn is sent only when the hub says the two see each other at that moment and the client does
    not hold the body.
  - A despawn is sent only when it holds the body and they do not.
  - Anything else is sent only when it holds the body.
  - Spawn records are read under that same lock.
- **So the order two goroutines reach a client in cannot leave it holding a body the hub does not
  show, or a stale state.** Every change of truth delivers after it changes.
- A single spawn built once for many recipients is rebuilt when the owner's state version moves under
  it (`bumpPlayerState`).
- A session with no view (put in the hub directly, as the unit tests and the benchmark's crowd setup
  do) hears everything in its window, as before.

**The broadcasts that were the zone's are now the window's:** motion, body mode, equipment, buffs,
and a cast's start and act (`zoneOf`, `equipObservers`). Chat, ground items and the monster
messages are unchanged: monsters have their own interest area since round 656.

**The client** (`client/scripts/net/user_spawn.gd`, `net_client.gd`; one line in `World3D.gd`):

- `UserSpawnNet` reads the CICUser record. It reads a group of any mix of NPC and PC records, telling
  them apart by RefObjID.
- Each record becomes the `WorldNet.EntitySnapshot` World3D already draws:
  - world coordinates by the server's `worldmap.ToWorld`, held equal to `World3D.region_local_to_world`
    by a test;
  - items by equip slot through the item table;
  - the heading from `RemoteFacing.from_wire_angle`.
- **A record cannot be read before the item table.**
  - The plus byte and the guild block depend on item types, as they do in the client's own reader.
  - So World3D hands its table to `NetClient.set_spawn_item_rows` in `_ready`. That is the one World3D
    line, right after `_load_character_assets`.
  - Until then, any group that holds a player waits in order, together with every spawn message
    behind it.
- While nobody listens (the world entry: CharacterSelect is still swapping World3D in), a spawned
  player joins `last_enter_world_result.others` and `seated`, which World3D lays out in `_ready`.
- After that, a spawn emits `entity_entered`, plus a motion `state_changed` for a sitter.
- A 0x3016 for a player emits `entity_left`, and drops its buffs and body mode, as `OpEntityLeft` did.
- `OpEntityEntered` / `OpEntityLeft` / the result lists stay decodable, for an older server.

### C2: encode once, write once (`server/internal/gateway`)

- `gateway.Frame` / `NewFrame` / `SendFrames`: a broadcast is encoded once per event. A session with
  per-session security still draws its own rolling byte and checksum, so the frame matches
  `Send`'s byte for byte. That is tested.
- The writer puts everything it drained on the socket in **one write**. Round 662's frame-per-write
  wedge was the client's to fix, and it is fixed: round 663's reader takes partial data.
- **The writer lingers `SendCoalesce` = 2 ms when woken to under 4 KB** (OURS, from the profile
  below), so a crowd's 26-byte moves go out many to a syscall.

### C4: the hub (`server/internal/world`)

- `EntityByCharacterID`, `FindByName`, `SetLifeState` and `SetLevel` are map lookups, not scans of
  every session.
- A move holds the write lock to set the position and refile the block. It copies out only the
  window's sessions, where it used to copy every entity in the zone.
- The player-view registry is a `sync.Map`, so a move's per-recipient lookups take no shared lock.

## C3 as a spec: move by destination

This was not built this round. It changes how every client walks, and it touches World3D, which
another lane is editing now. Every rule below is READ (`movement.md`, "0xB021 in full"). Only the
OpenSRO-side choices are marked OURS.

**The client sends.**

- On a ground click: `0x7021` = `01 | region u16 | x i16 | y i16 | z i16`, the raw local
  coordinates truncated, 9 bytes. One per accepted click; `World3D`'s own click filter is already
  retail's (round 637).
- On a sky click or the arrow keys: `0x7021` in the heading form, `00 | command u8 | angle u16`.
  While a heading run lasts, `0x7024 {u16 angle}` whenever the heading moves 1° or more.
- The 20 Hz `OpMove` stream goes. `OpMove` stays for relocations: QA, GM and the zone landing.

**The server accepts or refuses.**

- **Gates** (`GObjPC::FUN_004b0ea0`). Refuse silently when:
  - the character is dead;
  - its interaction state is not 0 or 1;
  - its motion state is CHANGEMOTION (0x12), 8 or 0x10;
  - its abnormal-state bits are `0xc1 | 0x4000`;
  - the destination's region is outside the 3 x 3 regions;
  - while moving, the destination is within 5.0 of the current one.
- **On accept:**
  - stand a sitter: SetMotionState WALK or RUN;
  - end the move-ended buffs (0xB072 each);
  - store `{source position, destination, start time, speed}` on the hub entity;
  - send **0xB021** to the 3 x 3, **the mover included**:
    `u32 uid | movement | u8 1 | src region u16 | x*10 u16 | y f32 | z*10 u16`.
    Flag 1 is always safe: the client's reader takes it, and retail's own test is a defect,
    INFERRED.

**The server's position (OURS model, in place of retail's server-side mover).**

- The position is `source + dir × min(speed × (now - start), distance)`. `speed` is the run speed
  with buffs, and the server's own cap is 160 a step, READ.
- An AOI tick (every 500 ms, OURS) relocates every moving player to its interpolated position.
  That is the existing `Relocate` -> `crossPlayer` path: a block crossing still spawns and despawns.
- **Walls.**
  - Retail's server steps the mover on its own navmesh, and sends **0xB023** (`u32 uid, u16 region,
    f32 x, y, z, u16 angle`, `0x487c80`) to the 3 x 3 when a step is refused.
  - OpenSRO's server has no navmesh for players. The client's walk stops at walls (round 653), so
    the client must report its stop.
  - Proposed (OURS): a stop report, `OpMove` with the relocated flag clear and a "stopped" bit, that
    the server takes when it is within the interpolation's reach (`distance(reported, expected) <=
    speed × 0.5 s`). It is then relayed as 0xB023.
  - This is the anti-cheat point. Today's server takes any position, so this is no weaker.
- **The spawn record** carries the destination form for a moving player:
  `01 | run flag | dest region u16 | i16 x, y, z` (`0x48be00`, READ). A late arrival then sees it
  walking. `pcspawn.Append` writes the no-destination form only today.

**The client walks others.**

- On 0xB021 for another player, set its walk target (MonsterMotion's leg stepper, already READ against
  retail's `CGObjMoverByDest`) and step it at its run speed every rendered frame. This is also lane B's
  B1.
- On flag 1, snap to the source first, then aim at the destination when it is more than 5 units away
  (`0x8a5750`).
- On its own 0xB021, the local player does the same.
- 0xB023 snaps.

**Bandwidth.** One 0xB021 per click instead of 20 `OpMove` a second: about 20x fewer move messages for
a walking crowd (INFERRED, from round 736's net20 numbers).

**Fix first:** `proto.DecodeMovement` reads a dungeon destination as f32. It is i32 (round 737's
correction, `movement.md`).

## Departures from retail (OURS), disclosed

- NPCs and players ride separate groups of a block. Retail's one group holds every object.
- Buffs follow a spawn as OpenSRO's `OpBuffStart`. The record's buff list is 0, because its entry
  width depends on skill rows the client has not loaded when it reads.
- A player in the sit-down transition is written as SIT (round 607's Seated rule). The engine writes
  0x12, and what its client does with that was not read.
- An arrival's 0x3015 is also sent to a client that already holds that character (a reconnect racing
  the old session's leave), so it redraws where it is.
- 0x3015's spawn type is 4 for an arrival and for a zone change too. Their own values are OPEN.
- The client classifies a record as a player's by the 52 creatable bodies' ids, not by TypeID.
- HwanLevel, the PVP cape, the job type and level, the guild and the stall are written in their plain
  forms. OpenSRO models a guild, but it is not in the record yet (OPEN).
- The 2 ms write linger.
