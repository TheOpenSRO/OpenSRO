### B4: what a body not yet dressed does

The node is in `_other_bodies` from its arrival, so every handler that acts on a player acts on it:

- **Moves** (`OpEntityMoved`, the probe's `move` and `net`): the node walks and turns as a dressed
  one; it is dressed where it has got to.
- **Departure**: it leaves the queue and is never dressed; its name comes from the node's
  `character_name` (it has no plate yet).
- **0x3038 / 0x3039**: `_redress_other` updates the queued items; the pluses are read from
  `_other_pluses` when it is dressed. So it is dressed with the item and plus of the latest
  broadcast.
- **Seats**: a result's Seated tail and `0x30bf` set `_other_seated`; it is dressed on the sitting
  loop without the sit-down clip, as `_spawn_result_others` seats a body drawn at once.
- **Buffs and berserk**: resynced when it is dressed, as before.
- A list (`_spawn_result_others`) is queued whole before the pump, so the frame's budget goes to
  the nearest of the list, not the first listed; the seats are applied before it.
- A lone arrival in a frame with budget left is dressed in its own call, as before.

Hooked at `_spawn_or_update_other` / the body build only; the packet parsing (lane C's) is
untouched.

## B5, written up instead of built

The spec's B5 is "merge each body's meshes, one per material" (expected: 8 draws a body down to
3-5). Not built, for two reasons:

- **As specified it saves nothing (MEASURED).** The probe's census now counts the distinct
  materials among each body's visible surfaces (`visible_materials`). At 20, 200 and 1000 it equals
  the visible meshes exactly: 155 / 155, 1,595 / 1,595, 7,994 / 7,994. Every visible part of a
  body has a material of its own, so one mesh per material is still 8 surfaces, and Godot issues a
  draw per surface: 8 draws a body either way.
- **It is lane A's code.** Merging skinned meshes needs one bind list for the merged mesh, which is
  A2 (one Skin per body, remapped bone indices at template time), and the merge itself would sit in
  `CharacterAppearance.build`, which round 738 is changing.

What would cut the draws, for whoever takes it:

- a per-body texture atlas, so one material covers several parts: the parts' textures packed at
  template time, their UVs remapped. It touches the materials `CharacterLight` swaps per level, the
  ones `EquipGlow` redraws, and `AppearFade`'s twins. INFERRED: 8 draws to 2-3 (body parts on one
  atlas, weapon and shield apart for their glow);
- A5 (spec): far bodies as `MultiMesh` instances per (part, material) with baked bone textures,
  which removes the per-body draw entirely.

## How to rerun

`client/tools/crowd_run.sh` (new) is r736's recipe plus a wait for no other lane's Godot and an
`overlap: N` count; on the box, from a shipped client, under the box lock:

```
flock /var/tmp/godot-lock/lock /opt/opensro-client-<lane>/tools/crowd_run.sh \
  /opt/opensro-client-<lane> /var/tmp/<lane>/n1000.log 1000 base,move,net,base [VAR=value ...]
```

It prints the `LINEUP:`, `QA CROWD arrival`, census, `QA CROWD step` and drain lines. This round's
boots ran its first version (`/var/tmp/r739/crowd.sh`, the same commands); the committed one was
checked with a 20-body boot. Added this round:

- `QA CROWD arrival n=.. frames=.. seconds=.. worst_ms=.. p50=.. p95=.. over100=..`: every frame's
  wall time from the spawn (less the lineup's own kit and ground work) until the last body is
  dressed; then the census and the steps.
- `World3D: N other player bod(ies) dressed over F frame(s) in T ms, longest frame .., p50 ..,
  p95 .., K over 100 ms (budget ..)`: the pump's own time a frame.
- `OPENSRO_QA_OTHER_BUILD_BUDGET_MS=<ms>` (QA only): another budget for the pump.
- The census's `visible_materials` (B5 above).
- The `shadow` switch now gives back only what it switched off (it used to turn every mesh's
  shadow on after, EquipFx's particles and Detail's switched-off bodies included).
- `QaLineup` caches its kits per (race, sex, weapon): `_kit` walks the whole item table, about 5 s
  for 1000 bodies, which r736's arrival time (10.3 s) included. The base side's arrival numbers
  above ran with the same cache patched into its copy (QA file only).
