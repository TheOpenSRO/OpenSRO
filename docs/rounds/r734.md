# Round 734 — weapon and shield glows (lane A: the client)

The spec is `docs/protocol/weapon_glow.md` (round 733). This round built its lane A: the client reads
the three tables, draws the `CRTModProgEquipPow` glow and the `+8` particles, and does so on every
dressed character. Labels: READ, MEASURED, INFERRED, OURS, OPEN.

## Built

- **`ItemGlowTables`** (`client/scripts/items/item_glow_tables.gd`) reads `Media.pk2
  resinfo\itemoption.txt`, `itemtypenumber.txt` and `itemoptionefp.txt` at runtime. The parsing is
  `0xa9f2e0`'s: CRLF, `//` comments, tab columns, and `atol` on the type number's `"1 "`. On the
  retail archive it reads 2,467 items with a type, 4 types with 44 rows and 2,468 items with
  particles, with 0 lines skipped (MEASURED, the perf run's log line).
  - `glow_row` returns the highest threshold ≤ plus, and nothing at +0. The plus is a u8.
  - `particle_rows` returns the rows with min ≤ plus ≤ max.
  - `pulse_t`, `tfactor` and `glow_colour` are the mod's arithmetic, for the tests and the
    measurement.
- **The glow** is the `EQUIP_GLOW` path of `object.gdshaderinc`. `EquipGlow.glow_shader` builds it
  as `unshaded` with the plain material's cull mode.
  - Stage 0 is `op0(optionTex(UV + fract(uv·50·TIME)), TF)`.
  - Stage 1 is `op1(itemTex, stage 0)`, clamped after each stage.
  - `TF = round(lerp(c1, c2, t)·255)/255`, where `t` is a triangle wave with period 2P that starts at
    colour 1 (READ this round, see below).
  - Flag 1 alpha-tests at 1/255. Flag 0 turns the test **off**, which READ (`vslot_004`: RS 15 = 0)
    says, rather than the spec's "keep the item's own".
  - There is one `ShaderMaterial` per (row, plain material, option texture). It keeps the plain
    material in meta, and `TIME` drives it, so there is no per-frame script. (Review fixes: one
    global clock, `srr_glow_clock`, replaced `TIME`; see "Review fixes".)
- **The gate** (READ: the glow needs the owner's colour word at full alpha). `AppearFade._make_twin`
  and the select screen's `_fade_pair` take `EquipGlow.plain_of(material)`. So a fading or
  translucent character draws its weapon plain.
- **Particles** are drawn per effect type by `EquipFx` (`client/scripts/assets/equip_fx.gd`, OURS,
  after the first gate; see Performance). A row whose bone the item lacks attaches nothing (READ).
  - There is one driver per viewport. It keeps one master `EfpEffect` per (efp, col3/100 scale),
    simulated at the origin, with its own draws hidden.
  - Each weapon showing that effect gets a holder. The holder's MultiMesh and Link children share the
    master's MultiMesh and meshes. The driver places it at the bone's position, scaled by col3/100,
    every frame.
  - Only the nearest 64 weapons in view within 800 units show their effect, re-chosen every 10
    frames.
  - **Differences from retail (all OURS):**
    - every weapon with the same effect is in step;
    - the particles follow the bone's position rigidly: there is no trail left behind while running,
      and the followers do not turn with the weapon;
    - the budget and the distance limit. Retail's limits (Effect Quality's reader, Character Sight
      Range's, `FUN_00c43780`) are not READ.
  - Standing still it looks the same as the per-weapon effect it replaced (frame-60 picture of the
    +10 bow compared before and after).
  - The per-weapon path `EfpEffect.effect_scale` (col3/100 on the anchor, READ via
    `CEFEffect::vslot_010`) remains for one-off use.
- **Where the glow applies.**
  - `CharacterAppearance.build` applies the glow to slots 6 and 7. It records them on the skeleton,
    and `reglow(skeleton, pluses)` redraws them without a rebuild.
  - **Your own character:** World3D takes `ItemInstance.effective_plus() & 0xff` per worn slot. This
    is OptLevel + the advanced elixir, READ (`CICPlayer::vslot_025` → item `+0x8c`; the
    coordinator's round-735 review agrees).
  - A plus change alone re-dresses the body.
  - The paper doll and the portrait get the glow too. The portrait drops the particles. (Untrue
    as first built: `PlayerPortrait._apply_materials` replaced the glow on the select screen, the
    paper doll and the portrait. True since the review fixes, below.)
  - **Other players:** `_spawn_or_update_other(..., scale, pluses)` takes slot → plus, which is lane
    B's hook. A dict given for a body already drawn reglows it.
  - **Character select** uses the list's plus. This is READ: `PSCharacterSelect::FUN_0085a580` →
    `InstallEquipVisual(slot, id, plus, 0)`.
- **QA hooks:**
  - `OPENSRO_QA_EQUIP="6=101+9"` dresses your character with a plus.
  - `OPENSRO_QA_LINEUP_KITS="6=101+13,7=281+13;..."` spawns one other-player body per kit.
  - `OPENSRO_QA_LINEUP_SPACING`, and `OPENSRO_QA_FAKECHARS_PLUS` for the select screen's QA list.
- **The measurement tool** is `client/tools/measure_equip_glow.gd`.

## Review fixes (`docs/rounds/r734_review.md`)

Labels as above. Each fix has a test; the suites are listed at the end.

- **F1: the select screen, the equipment window and the portrait keep the glow.**
  `PlayerPortrait._portrait_material` no longer turns a glow material into a plain lit one. It returns
  `EquipGlow.view_copy`: a copy of the glow material with every uniform, standing in for the view's
  own material for the item's plain layer (`PLAIN_META`). That plain material keeps the layer's
  alpha reference and takes the view's light.
  - The equipment window and the portrait draw their SubViewports with the Linear tone map, so their
    copy is on a variant of the glow shader, `glow_shader(cull, test, present = false)`. The variant
    defines `OBJECT_NO_PRESENT` and writes the glow without `srr_obj_present`, whose global
    `srr_obj_tonemap` belongs to the world.
  - The select screen's bodies stand in the world's Filmic environment. Its `PlayerPortrait` sets
    `glow_world_present`, so its copy is on the world's glow shader. The copy is not in the view's
    `_materials`, so `_undo_tonemap` and the light push leave it alone. The fade (`_fade_pair` takes
    `plain_of`) fades the view's plain material, as before.
  - The test is the review's probe A (`item_glow_tables_test`, `_test_view_glow`). A flag-0 (+3
    shield) and a flag-1 (+13 sword) glow over a plain layer with `alpha_scissor` 0.502 go through
    `_apply_materials` in both modes. After it, `is_glow` is true, on the expected shader, with the
    row's uniforms, and `plain_of(...).alpha_ref` is 0.502. On `cf11fefa` it was `is_glow` false and
    `alpha_ref` 0.
  - The claims at the top of this file and in `weapon_glow.md` §4 and §8 item 10 are corrected to
    match.
- **F2: a plus-only `0x3038` re-glows the standing body.** In `_on_other_equipment_changed`, when
  the slot already holds the same item, the new `_reglow_other` redraws the glow
  (`CharacterAppearance.reglow`) instead of calling `_redress_other`. It falls back to a rebuild
  only when no body is drawn. `_spawn_or_update_other` uses the same helper. Test:
  `equip_arrival_test`, `_test_plus_only`: the body is kept and the wear is redrawn at +13; another
  item rebuilds; +0 takes the glow off.

  frame chooses (OURS).
