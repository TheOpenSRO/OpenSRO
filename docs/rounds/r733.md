# Round 733 — weapon and shield glows (spec)

The user (2026-10-05) asked for the +N weapon glows and the particle effects, including different
ones for Seal of Star, Moon, Sun and Nova, "fully supported". This round is the spec only. Nothing
changes in behaviour. The full findings are in `docs/protocol/weapon_glow.md`.

## Found

- **Two effects, both keyed by the plus.**
  - The `CRTModProgEquipPow` glow, from +3. Its tiers are +3, +5, +6, +7, +8, +9, +10, +11, +12, +13
    and +14, and the highest threshold ≤ plus wins (READ, `0xa93900`).
  - An `.efp` on a weapon bone, from +8 (MEASURED: all 2,468 rows of `itemoptionefp.txt` have min 8
    and max 100).
- **The three tables** live in `Media.pk2 resinfo\`: `itemoption.txt` (item → type 1–4),
  `itemtypenumber.txt` (44 rows) and `itemoptionefp.txt`. They are loaded by
  `UI_ALCHEMYBOX::FUN_00a9f2e0` (READ). Every type has the same colour at a given tier. The type
  changes only the UV scroll direction and speed and the alpha-test flag (MEASURED).
- **The glow pass** (READ, `vslot_002/004/005`):
  - stage 0 is the option texture × TFACTOR (MODULATE at +3, MODULATE2X above);
  - stage 1 ADDs (ADDSIGNED2X at +13) a texture whose alpha drives the alpha test;
  - TFACTOR is a triangle wave between the row's two colours over `period` ms;
  - the UV scrolls at `uv × 50`/s. `_DAT_00e1b830` = 20.0 and `_DAT_00d83108` = 255.0, MEASURED in
    `.rdata`;
  - it is drawn only at full alpha.
- **Seals do not choose an effect** (MEASURED). Nova, Star, Moon, Sun and the honor seals use the
  normal item's glow type, particle file, scale and model. Only the Egyptian set items differ: glow
  type 1, and particle scales 185 and 215 against 175. Only their `_SWITCHnn` variants have particle
  files of their own (`*_set_a_rare_01` and `*_set_b_rare_01`). This contradicts the user's
  expectation, and is gap 1 for the user.
- **The retail data path:** `0x3038` carries u8 OptLevel after the RefItemID for equipment (READ,
  `FUN_008a9260`).
- **OpenSRO's data path:** the entity snapshot carries no plus, and there is no equip-change broadcast
  to observers (MEASURED in the tree).
- **No Option-window gate** is on the attach or mod path (READ). Whether particle quality gates the
  `.efp` is OPEN.

## Lanes

- **A (client):** the readers, the shader, particles on the bones, and every view of the character.
  Live renders measured at +0, +3, +5, +7, +9 and +13/+14, the seal comparison, and the perf gate.
- **B (server + net):** the plus in a trailing snapshot block, and retail `0x3038`/`0x3039`
  broadcasts to observers. Go and GD tests, and a two-client live check.

The full list is §8 of the spec.
