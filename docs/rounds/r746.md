## Finding 1: commands along the world's axes turned with the bone

**Which effects (MEASURED).** `client/tools/census_equip_world.gd` reads every particle file the
two tables name (`itemrare.txt` and `itemoptionefp.txt`, through `ItemGlowTables`: 74 files). It
counts the commands whose vector or rotation is applied along the WORLD's axes. The rules are READ
(EFP_FORMAT.md, "What the space selects"); `EquipFx.world_commands` applies them:

| Command | World-axis when | Why |
|---|---|---|
| SetPosition, SetConePos, SetBANPos | space < 3 (r = 0), value not zero | the value is not turned by any frame |
| SetVelocity, SetConeVel, Force, ConeForce | space 0, value not zero | `_frame(p, 0)` is the identity |
| SetRotation*, SetBANRot | space 0, not the identity | an absolute rotation |
| SetRVelocity* | not the identity | applied after the rotation, in the outer frame |
| SetSpherePos | space 0; or a scale that differs per axis | the ball's axes are the world's |

The result: **35 of the 74 files.** The review counted 10. It missed the per-axis `SetSpherePos`
commands, which add 25 files. Most have the value `(1, 0, 1)`: a disc in the world's horizontal
plane.

| Command and space | Commands (schedule entries) | Example files |
|---|---|---|
| `SetConeVel` space 0 | 28 | `eternity_11_nova_01`, `nova_bow_02/03`, `nova_shield_02/03`, `egya_sealhusk_purple`, `egyb_coreglow_green`, `egyb_sealhusk_green` (the review's 8 files; it counted 56 with each body list's controller copy) |
| `SetRVelocity` space 0 | 1 | `system_rarestaff_a` |
| `SetSpherePos` space 1 with a per-axis scale | 59 | `(1, 0, 1)` ×37, `(1.5, 0, 1.5)` ×5, `(0.5, 0.5, 1)` ×4, `(0.5, 0, 0.5)` ×4, `(2, 1.3, 1.3)` ×3, `(2, 0, 2)` ×3, `(1.51, 1.5, 1.5)` ×2, `(3, 1, 3)` ×1, in 31 files: the Sun seal `raretype_a_step3`, the Star seal `raretype_a_step1`, the bow and shield seals, the Nova and Egyptian files |
| `SetPosition` space 0 | 0 that matter | the review's 2 in `egyb_coreglow_green_shield` have the zero vector |

**Fix.**
- A kind whose template has a world-axis command (`Kind.own`) still has its shared master.
- The **nearest weapon** that carries one (`EquipFx.OWN_BUDGET` = 1, OURS) runs each such effect
  on its own simulation: `EquipFx._own_holder`, the per-weapon path. While the camera follows the
  player, that is the player's own weapon, the one the user looks at; the user has a Nova weapon.
  - It is placed every frame at the bone's whole frame (`follow_bone_full`, READ
    `CRTModParticle::FUN_00c81190`), at the row's scale.
  - It is simulated in a space that does not turn with the bone, as retail does. So a space-0
    vector points along the world's axes.
- Every other weapon draws the effect from the shared master, turned with the bone (OURS: the
  review's departure, kept away from the player's weapon).
- A weapon's world-axis effects stay on one path together: the own budget counts weapons, not
  effects.

**Why 1 (MEASURED).** The first version allowed 4 own weapons.
- `client/tools/profile_equip_crowd.gd` (200 Sun spears, headless, 300 frames): script time 3.63
  ms a frame with no own simulation, against 7.33 ms with 4. An own effect places itself, steps,
  rewrites its draws and rebuilds its pipes every frame while the hand moves.
- The 200-Sun-spear gate with 4 went 21.9 → 28.4 ms a frame (below).
- With 1 (200 Nova spears; the player-side weapon carries 2 own effects): 5.16 ms against 4.55 ms
  with none, **+0.6 ms**.

**Measured** (`measure_equip_fx_turn.gd`, 8 headings with the hands swaying):
- The own simulations sit on the weapon:
  - nova_01: along [4.00, 19.41], off the axis 2.91 at most;
  - raretype_a_step3: [−1.86, 10.73], 4.01.
  - The shared path gave about the same ranges.
- The centroid in the item's own frame now moves a little between headings: 0.39 for raretype,
  1.76 for nova_01, against 0.000 for the shared path. That is the world-axis part, which no longer
  turns with the weapon. It is also the own simulations' own random seeds.
- The enchant effect (no world-axis command) still sits 0.005 off the blade, with a spread of 0.000.

## Finding 2: the budget cuts by whole weapons

**Decision (OURS; retail's limits are not READ):** the budget still caps the holders drawn at
`VISIBLE_BUDGET` = 64, and it now cuts by whole weapons (item skeletons).
- `_finish_cycle` takes one distance per weapon and ranks the weapons.
- It shows the nearest weapons whose effects together fit 64 holders. A weapon never shows half its
  effects.
- `stats["weapons"]` counts the weapons shown.

**Why not 64 weapons, as recommended (MEASURED):** the first version of this round counted 64
weapons. With 200 Sun spears (two effects each) that drew 128 holders, against 64 on dev, and cost
**+8.2 ms a frame**: 21.99 → 30.18 ms, SLOWER (table below). Capping by holders keeps dev's draw
cost. The trade is that 32 Sun spears, or 16 Nova weapons, show their effects in a dense crowd,
where dev showed half the effects of 64. If the user prefers more weapons at that cost, this is the
setting to raise.

Test `equip_fx_test._test_weapon_budget`: 80 weapons with 2 effects each give 64 holders, 32 whole
weapons, the nearest, with none half-shown.

## Finding 3: group names resolve as item codes

`ItemGlowTables.inherit` keeps the code names of every item it is given. `_rebuild_types` and
`_rebuild_particles` skip a group whose name is not one of them (READ, the review: `FUN_00aa0270`
via `FUN_004f19f0`, and `FUN_00a9f2e0`).
- A name item that arrives later, with a fuller table, resolves then.
- On the retail archive nothing changes. `glow_inherit_probe.gd` gives 496 members in 496 groups,
  **480** inherit a type and 480 a particle list, the same as round 740.
- Tests in `item_glow_tables_test`: a group named by a code no item has gives no type and no list,
  and a later item resolves the group. The existing cases got their name items.

## Findings 5 and 6: the preload's wait, and the boot log line

- `ItemTableReader.wait_pending()` waits for a preload under way and keeps its rows as the shared
  ones.
  - `World3D._exit_tree` calls it, and `CharTables.wait_pending()`, so a world that leaves before it
    asked leaves no worker behind.
  - `load_all` for another archive also waits for it first.
- `ItemTableReader.last_source` is "shared", "preload" or "parsed". The boot line prints the parse
  numbers only when this call's rows were parsed now or by this world's preload. Otherwise it says
  "shared (parsed before this world)".
- Test `char_tables_test`: wait_pending then load_all gives "shared"; a waited load_all gives
  "preload"; a cold load_all gives "parsed".

**4,576 codes** with a model were missing from the old list, **670 of them `MOB_`**. With the

**Fix: `CharTables`** (`client/scripts/npc/char_tables.gd`).
- `table_paths` reads the index (`characterdata.txt`). `NpcTableReader.LEGACY_MODEL_TABLE_PATHS`
  is the fallback, and `NpcTableReader.model_table_paths` points to `table_paths`.
- Two worker tasks start at the top of `World3D._ready`:
  - **boot**: speeds, Country and the bodies' catalogue. `_load_character_assets` waits for it.
  - **entry**: BCRadius, DecayTime, the NPC models, codes and names, the monster models by code,
    the Org codes, the models by name, and the object-name table. The NPC and monster loaders take
    it, or `_adopt_char_entry` takes it when the entry's grid is in, before anything spawns.
    BCRadius and DecayTime were read at boot before; nothing uses them before a character or
    monster spawns.
- Every reader is the same as before, on the same bytes. Only the part list and the thread
  changed.
- The name-key index (already on a worker), the select screen's preview and 12 tools read
  `table_paths`.
- Test: `char_tables_test` (synthetic archive).
- **Live:** `World3D: NPC tables -- 9999 model(s), 15890 name(s)`, against `5423 model(s), 13169
  name(s)` in round 741's render.

nearest weapon's two effects adds 2.3 ms, measured as an A/B on one build.

  re-places, redraws and rebuilds its pipes every frame while the hand moves.
- At 45 frames a second the effect steps nearly every frame. At the 650 frames a second of the
  plain Jangan view, a step falls every 33 frames. That is why the player's own Nova spear alone
  costs nothing measurable (1.54 → 1.54) and a 200-body crowd shows it.
