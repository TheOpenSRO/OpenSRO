# Round 741, two bugs from testing v0.1.501: invisible items, and weapon particles beside the weapon

The user tested v0.1.501 (the weapon glows, rounds 733-735). Two bugs:

1. Item 62607, `ITEM_CH_SPEAR_11_A_RARE_ACTIVE` "Reaper Soul Spear" (Seal of Nova), had no icon, no
   name and no model in the hand.
2. At +9, `ITEM_CH_SPEAR_11_C_RARE` (4118, Seal of Sun) drew its particles a few units beside the
   spear: a column of sparks next to the shaft, seen from above. They lined up with the spear only
   "if I look directly south east". The user says the original shows "electricity plus a radiating
   sphere at the weapon tip".

This branch merges round 740 (`a5408848`: glow and particle inheritance through OrgObjCodeName, and
`resinfo\itemrare.txt`, the seal particles). Labels: **READ** (the client's decompile or
disassembly, address given), **MEASURED** (the retail archives on the render box), **INFERRED**,
**OURS**, **OPEN**.

## Bug 1: the item table read 7 of the archive's 63 parts

**Cause (MEASURED).**
- `ItemTableReader.TABLE_PATHS` hard-coded seven paths: the index file `itemdata.txt` itself,
  and the parts `_5000` to `_30000`.
- The archive's own index, `server_dep\silkroad\textdata\itemdata.txt`, names 63 parts:
  `ItemData_1000.txt` to `ItemData_63000.txt`, in that order.
- Item 62607 is in `itemdata_45000.txt`.
- Every consumer resolves items through those rows, so a missing row breaks all of them:
  - the world's `_item_rows` and `_item_models`;
  - the HUD's `_items`, which gives the inventory icon, the name, the tooltip and the shop;
  - the select screen's preview;
  - `CharacterAppearance`'s rows, which the world handed in, so it never ran its own full load.

**What the archive holds** (`client/tools/probe_table_parts.gd`; GDScript readers, because the box
has no native library):

| | parts | bytes | item rows | parse |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| the old list | 7 | 15,622,584 | 11,772 | 711 ms |
| the index's list | 63 | 39,162,904 | **15,338** | 1,691 ms |

- The index's list adds **3,566 items** that no old part defines.
- No old item is lost.
- 0 shared ids change their winning row between the two orders.
- `itemdata_55500.txt` is in the archive, but no index line names it, so it is not read (neither
  is it by the client).

**Item 62607 (MEASURED, the probe and the text table):**
- code `ITEM_CH_SPEAR_11_A_RARE_ACTIVE`;

  archive with no index.
- `ItemTableReader.load_all(media)` parses every part once per archive, keyed by the file's path

  - `World3D._load_character_assets`;
  - `HudPanels._load_items` (the inventory, names, tooltip and shop);
  - `CharacterPreview`;
  - `CharacterAppearance._item_info`.
  Before this, the world and the HUD each parsed the seven parts. The select screen's
  appearance already parsed all 63 when it met an unknown id.
- The tools that read the old list use `table_paths`: `dump_item_set`, `portrait_shot`,
  `check_item_requirements`, `census_item_envmap`, `probe_equipment`, `table_parity`,
  `probe_table_cost` and round 740's `glow_inherit_probe`.

## Bug 2: the weapon particles beside the weapon

### Cause 1: the holder took the bone's position, not its frame

Retail's placement is READ in `CRTModParticle::FUN_00c81190` (disassembly `0xc81190..0xc8134c`):

- **With a bone** (`+0x50` non-zero, `0xc811c5`): it takes the bone object's matrix
  (`[+0x50]+0x80`, then `+0x40`) and multiplies it by the owner's world matrix (vtable `+0x18` of
  `+0x5c`) through `0x55ebd0`.
- **Without a bone** (`0xc811e5`): a translation by the row's offset times the scale (`+0x94..+0x9c`
  × `+0xc0`), times the world matrix.
- When the mod's `+8` byte is set, the rotation part is first multiplied by the mod's own matrix at
  `+0xc`, and the translation is kept.
- Then `vslot_010(+0xc0)` sets the effect's scale, and `vslot_007` (`+0x1c`) hands it the matrix.
- So **the effect's frame is the bone's frame**, rotation included.

`ai_end` is the right bone, and on the item's own skeleton. `EquipFx.attach` hangs each row on the
item's skeleton, which keeps the `.bsk` bones (round 734, OPEN 12).

Round 734's `EquipFx` put each holder at the bone's position only, with no rotation. The +8 effect,
`system\system_enchant_a_01.efp`, places its sparks through five `SetPosition` nodes:
- space 7, which is the parent's position plus the value turned by the parent's rotation;
- values 0, −1.2, −2.4, −3.6 and −4.8 along the effect's X (MEASURED, `efpcensus -dumpone`).

Without the bone's rotation those offsets ran along a fixed world axis. That gave a column of
sparks beside the spear. The column lies over the shaft only when the view looks along that axis,
which fits the user's "south east" (INFERRED: a fixed world-direction offset, seen end-on).

### Cause 2: one effect per bone

`EquipFx` keyed a host by `skeleton|bone`. A seal weapon now has two rows on one bone:
- its `itemrare.txt` seal row (round 740);
- from +8, its `itemoptionefp.txt` row.

For the Sun spear 4118 these are `system_raretype_a_step3.efp` and `system_enchant_a_01.efp`, both
on `ai_end`. The second row was dropped. Retail makes a mod for each entry (`FUN_00a5f3e0`'s loop,
READ in round 733). Hosts are now keyed `skeleton|bone|effect`.

### Fix

- **`EquipFx.holder_transform(skel, bone, row_scale)`**:
  - the holder sits in the bone's world frame, `skel.global_transform * bone_global_pose`;
  - its basis is orthonormalised, keeping a mirror, times col3/100 times the host's uniform
    scale (round 734's F4);
  - every shown holder is placed this way every frame.
- **`EfpEffect.use_shared_turn()`** (OURS), called on each `EquipFx` master. The master's
  instances are shared by every holder, so anything that depends on the camera moves into the
  vertex shader:
  - **Billboard plates** (`ViewBillboard`, `ViewYBillboard`):
    - the instance carries the particle's scale as its basis and its position as its origin;
    - the custom data carries the shape's rotation as a quaternion (`EfpEffect.turn_custom`);
    - `TURN_VERTEX` scales the quad by the instance matrix's column lengths (the holder's
      uniform scale is in them), turns it by the shape, lays it on the camera's right, up (world
      up for YBillboard, READ) and look (`EfpEffect.view_basis`'s basis), and puts it at the
      turned position;
    - so the billboard faces the camera whatever the holder's turn.
  - **Pipes** (LinkPipe, LinkDPipe):
    - `EfpLinks.pipe_turn` writes each point twice, as its centre, with the neighbours
      `pipe_ribbon` projects and the signed width in two custom channels;
    - `PIPE_TURN_VERTEX` projects the neighbours with the camera that draws the holder and builds
      the side, `right·d.y − up·d.x`, scaled to the width (READ, `EFEffect::FUN_00c8d0e0`, as
      `pipe_ribbon` does);
    - the projection's off-centre terms cancel in the difference, and its signs are dropped so a
      Y-flipped projection draws the same side (OURS).
  - **ViewNone meshes and LinkObj** need nothing: they turn with the holder, as their particles'
    rotation follows the effect's (u3).
  - **A billboard with a TextureSlide** keeps the old path. Its custom data is its UV rectangle,
    so its billboard turns with the holder. None exists among the 74 files (MEASURED).
  - **Bonus:** the masters no longer rewrite their MultiMeshes or pipe strips when only the camera
    turns. Before, every camera move rewrote every billboard node.
- **`EquipFx.add_host`** keys hosts by bone and effect (cause 2).

**Differences from retail, all OURS:**
- every weapon with the same effect is in step;
- a velocity that retail keeps in the world turns with the holder (`u4 = 0` nodes with `Force`
  in the seal files). This differs only while the weapon turns during a particle's short life;
- the budget is unchanged (64 nearest in view within 800 units).
