# Round 744: standing on Karakoram's frozen lake

The user reported: "there's a big ice lake in Karakoram that you're supposed to be able to walk on,
but you end up just going right through it."

Labels: **READ** (decompile or disassembly), **MEASURED** (the real files on the render box,
nothing shipped), **OURS**.

## Where the lake is (MEASURED)

`textzonename.txt` names the regions. The frozen area is "Karakoram Ancient Remains" (described as
the zone the goddess froze) and the "Karakoram Oasis" next to it.

`waterat` over 84-96/120-131:
- Every block of 88/126-129, 89/125-129, 90/124-129, 91/124-129 and 92/126-129 has `.m`
  PlaneType 1, the still surface MapmWater draws, at plane **800**.
- The edges are partial: 87/127-128, 88/125, 88/130, 89/130, 92/125 and 94/124-125.

Under the ice, the ground is far below the surface. In 90/127 the blocks run from 396 to 837, and

arithmetic is `worldmap.ToWorld` in Hotan's frame (92/135).

## Why the player went through (READ and MEASURED)

**What the client did.** The walk took its ground from `NvmCollision.sample_height`. That is the
heightmap alone, through `NavGraph.step_tracked`, `surface_at`, `landing_height_at` and
`heights_at`. Nothing read the `.nvm` PlaneTypeMap except the old water drawing. So on the lake
the player stood on the lakebed, 260-300 units under the drawn ice. The click ray also hit only
the heightfield (`_ground_point_at`, terrain layer), so a click on the ice landed on the lakebed
far beyond it.

**The heights are not the cause.** The `.nvm` heightmap equals the `.m` one on all 9,409 vertices
of 90/127, 89/127 and 88/125 (a scratch comparison, MEASURED). The surface is not in the
heightmap; it is the plane.

**The original's rule (READ).** `CRTNavMeshTerrain::vslot_001` (0x004051b0) is the terrain's
height query:
1. It interpolates the heightmap (+0x1205c) at `_ftol(x/20)`, `_ftol(z/20)`.
2. It takes `row = tile_z >> 4`, `col = tile_x >> 4`.
3. It tests `PlaneTypeMap[row*6 + col] & 2` (+0x1b360, `test BYTE PTR [edx+esi],0x2` at
   0x405319).
4. When the bit is set and `PlaneHeight` (+0x1b384) is above the ground, y becomes the plane
   (`fcom`, `test ah,0x41`, `fstp [edi+4]`, 0x405333-0x405347).

The only other reader of the map is `FUN_00403980`, the matching ground normal, which returns a
flat one on the plane. The parser that fills those offsets is `RTNavMeshTerrain::FUN_00403450`.

**Which blocks carry the bit (MEASURED, `waterat -census`, all 4,704 regions).** `.nvm` type 3 on
844 blocks and type 2 on 111. Together they are exactly the 955 `.m` PlaneType-1 blocks; type 2 is
the WaveID-0, undrawn ones. Plain water is type 1 (35,045 blocks) and has no floor, so it stays
wadeable as before.

**Server.** The server has no navmesh and takes positions from the client (`session/buffs.go`
says so at `moveBySkill`), so it had nothing to agree with. No server change.

## The fix

- `NvmCollision` (`client/scripts/nvm/nvm_collision.gd`):
  - `SOLID_PLANE_BIT` (2), `plane_index` and `solid_plane_height` (all READ).
  - `terrain_height`: the heightmap raised to the block's floor, retail's `vslot_001`.
  - `solid_plane_faces`: one 320 x 320 quad per floor block, at its PlaneHeight.
  - `build_wall_triangles` now bases the walls on `terrain_height`.
- `NavGraph`: every terrain height (`heights_at`, `surface_at`, `landing_height_at`, and
  `step_tracked`'s exit and terrain cases) is `terrain_height`. So are Ghost Walk's landing and the
  QA COLUMN report.
- `World3D`: `_prepare_navmesh_walls` builds the floor quads on the prepare task. The commit's new
  `_add_solid_planes` adds them as a two-sided `ConcavePolygonShape3D` on `TERRAIN_COLLISION_LAYER`
  (body `SolidPlanes`). Click-to-move, the camera arm, the snap and monster and ground-item
  placement then all stop at the ice. **OURS:** that retail's pick hits the plane was not read;
  only its height rule was. The click ring needed nothing: `GroundMarkers` already draws retail's
  flat quad at the point's height over a still-water block.
- Docs:
  - `docs/protocol/NVM_FORMAT.md`: new section "PlaneTypeMap: bit 1 is a floor", with the census
    table.
  - `docs/protocol/MAPM_FORMAT.md`: a pointer to it from the water census.
