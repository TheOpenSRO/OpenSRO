# Round 747 — crowd scale, lane A: A5, the far crowd on the GPU

The spec is `docs/protocol/crowd_gpu.md` (round 738). The target (`crowd_scale.md` section 4, OURS):
1000 other players in view at 1920 x 1080, Middle, standing p50 at most 16.7 ms and walking p95 at
most 33 ms. Where it stood (round 739's combined table): 1000 standing about 95 ms, walking p95 184.

Labels: READ (our code, or Godot 4.2.2's source at `4.2.2-stable`), MEASURED (the box: RX 7600,
RADV NAVI33, Forward+ through `xwl_run.sh`, unless it says Compatibility), COMPUTED, INFERRED, OURS,
OPEN.

Base: `daeb1b0c` (dev, v0.1.508).

## 1. The probe first (crowd_gpu.md OPEN 1)

**Question:** does a `MultiMesh` draw of a skinned surface get its bones and weights in a shader
that reads `BONE_INDICES` / `BONE_WEIGHTS`, with no skeleton?

**READ (Godot 4.2.2):** a surface's vertex format binds `ARRAY_BONES` / `ARRAY_WEIGHTS` from its
`skin_buffer` (`R16G16B16A16_UINT` / `_UNORM`) whenever the shader's input mask asks for them
(`mesh_storage.cpp:1301-1318`); with no mesh instance (`mis == null`, a MultiMesh draw) the vertex
buffer is the surface's own, unskinned (`:1244-1248`). Every skinned surface has a skin buffer
(`:399`). So the route is the spec's: the vertex shader skins from a bone texture.

**MEASURED, both renderers, the real Figures** (`client/tools/crowd_gpu_probe.gd`, section 4 below):
yes. The far draw of 8 Figures (+13 kit, Volume 0/2/4, running) lands on the near draw's pixels:
Forward+ `d2=0.00002 dmax=215` (about 18 pixels of 921,600 differ by more than 2, all on
silhouette edges), Compatibility `d2=0.00001`. Neither log has a `SHADER ERROR`.

Two things the probe found that the spec did not have:

- **The normal.** For an instance whose scale is uniform, Godot's `MODEL_NORMAL_MATRIX` is
  `mat3(MODEL_MATRIX)` itself, not its inverse transpose (`scene_forward_clustered.glsl:210-214`;
  Compatibility `scene.glsl:318-323`, READ). A near body at Height 1.06 has its normal scaled by 1.06,
  which the object shader's `N.L` keeps. The first far build divided it out and drew the 0x44 body
  measurably darker (`d8=0.003` on 8 bodies); multiplying by the body's basis, as Godot does, matched.
- **Out of the tree a pose change does not mark a skeleton dirty** (`skeleton_3d.cpp:575`, READ), so
  the bake's scratch skeleton is composed with `force_update_all_bone_transforms()`; without it every
  baked frame was the first one (the first CPU check: 31 units off).

## 2. Built

All OURS unless marked.

### CrowdGpu (`client/scripts/world/crowd_gpu.gd`)

A far body's skeleton is hidden and left unposed, its clip entry leaves World3D's clip tick, and each
visible mesh part becomes one instance of a MultiMesh shared by every far body, keyed by (mesh,
material, layers, sorting offset). The instance's material is a **twin** of the body's own: the same
shader code with `#define CROWD_GPU`, the crowd's uniforms (`crowd_gpu.gdshaderinc`) and the skin at
the top of `vertex()` (`crowd_gpu_vertex.gdshaderinc`; the object shaders include it from
`object.gdshaderinc`'s own `vertex()` under `CROWD_GPU`), and every parameter copied by name.

Three textures, RGBA32F, read with `texelFetch`:

- **Bones, one per skeleton layout** (`MonsterCrowd.META_LAYOUT`: the two skeleton files). A row per
  baked frame, 3 texels a bone: the skin matrix `pose_global x bind`, with the bind the shared skin's
  (`Skeleton3DBuilder.canonical_skin`). The frames are CrowdAnim's own A3 frames
  (`CrowdAnim.frames_for`, `QUANTUM_MS` = 1000/120), so a far body shows exactly the frame a near
  body would. Untracked bones sit at rest, as a built body's do (`Skeleton3DBuilder.build_skeleton3d`
  resets every pose, READ).
  - Baked lazily, a clip at a time, 4 ms of a frame at most; the whole texture is uploaded at most
    every 15 frames while clips bake (Godot 4.2 has no partial 2-D update), and a clip is drawn from
    only once its rows are up. A clip a body waits for goes before the runs baked ahead.
  - A far body's run is baked ahead when it goes far, so its first step does not bring it back.
- **Bodies:** 4 texels a body: its skeleton's global transform, and (first row, frame count,
  duration, offset). The shader picks the frame `CrowdAnim.frame_index` picks from one global
  uniform, `srr_crowd_clock` (ms since an epoch moved every 60 s, so a float keeps 0.004 ms of
  precision): no per-body clip tick, and a standing body is never written again.
- **Volume:** per layout, a row per Volume variant skin in use, each bone's `rest x variant bind`;
  a variant's skin is the baked matrix times it.

**Height** is in the body transform. **Items with their own skeleton** (weapons, shields, shoulder
pieces) and rigid pieces hang from a body bone through a `BoneAttachment3D`: their vertices are moved
once (`CrowdGpu.rigid_mesh`, cached per mesh, layout and bone) into that bone's rest space -- through
the item skeleton's own skin at rest, as Godot's pass would -- and weighted to that bone alone. That is
the attachment's composition (`pose_global(attach) x chain x vertex`) with `pose_global = skin x rest`.

Per-instance variation:

- **Equipment:** each worn mesh is its own group; nothing per instance.
- **CharacterLight's levels** (crowd_gpu.md OPEN 3): a level is a material copy, so a twin per copy and
  a group per (mesh, level). `CharacterLight.set_on` reports a change (`on_changed`), and a far body's
  parts are re-read (`CrowdGpu.refresh`).
- **The glow** (OPEN 4): `EquipGlow`'s shader is an object shader with `EQUIP_GLOW`; its twin is the
  same code with the skin, on the same `srr_glow_clock`. Drawn far, identical at a held clock
  (section 4). Weapon **particles** are not drawn on far bodies: EquipFx's budget already shows the
  64 weapons nearest the camera (round 734), it skips a weapon whose skeleton is hidden, and the
  near set is those 64 bodies.
- **Character Sight Range:** in the vertex shader, against the body's axis at the part's rest centre
  height (Godot cuts a near part by its bounds' centre: OURS approximation, within a part's half width).

Culling: the MultiMesh is culled as a whole by one AABB around every far body (grown as they move,
re-measured each cycle). Far instances cast no shadow (Detail's ten casters are near).

While far, the skeleton is `top_level`: a walking holder no longer sends each hidden piece's
transform to the server every frame (`Node3D._propagate_transform_changed` skips a top_level child;
`VisualInstance3D` sends `instance_set_transform` on every change, shown or not: `node_3d.cpp:113`,
`visual_instance_3d.cpp:58-61`, READ). Its place is the holder's times its own transform.

### World3D: which bodies are far

- Every 10 frames, from a scan spread over them: the 64 bodies nearest the camera in view stay near
  (`CrowdGpu.near_count`, OURS: EquipFx's 64 and Detail's ten need posed skeletons). A near body goes
  far only once it is out of the nearest 80 (`NEAR_HYSTERESIS` 1.25, by rank, OURS). Out-of-view
  bodies are far.
- Kept near whatever their rank (`_crowd_eligible`): a body still dressing, fading in or out
  (AppearFade), berserk (BerserkLook tints its meshes), casting Detail's shadow, or carrying an
  effect (EfpEffect follows a bone of the posed skeleton).
- Switching to the GPU: at most 3 ms a frame (`SWITCH_BUDGET_USEC`).
- **Brought back at once** (`_crowd_wake`), posed at its clip's current frame, which is the frame the
  GPU drew: a one-shot (`_play_once`: hits, sitting down, attacks), a loop the GPU has not baked
  (`_swap_other_loop`), berserk, an aura (`_fx_owner_body`), a new glow (`_reglow_other`), Detail
  choosing it (`CharacterBlobs.on_cast`), a redress (the old skeleton), a departure (it fades on the
  full path), a skeleton leaving the tree (`tree_exiting`), A5 switched off.
- A loop change of a far body (stand / run / sit) is a texel write when baked (`_swap_other_loop`).
- A far body's clip sounds rest while it is far (`AnimSounds.rest`), OURS: the GPU path advances none.
  Within 600 of the listener (the READ hearing rule) the near set still sounds; each sound plays on at
  most 4 voices (`SoundTable.VOICES_PER_SOUND`, OURS) either way.

### QA and tools

- `QaCrowdProbe`: `anim=` steps take `5` (A5), and restore the boot's switches after the step; an `ab`
  step (the same frame with A5 on, on again, and off, at held clip, glow and texture clocks); each step
  line ends with CrowdGpu's census and `gpu_ms` / `others_ms`.
- `OPENSRO_QA_CROWD_GPU=0` boots without A5; `OPENSRO_QA_CROWD_NEAR=<n>` sets the near count.
- `client/tools/crowd_gpu_probe.gd` (section 1, 4); `frame_ab.py` prints `d2` and `dmax` too.
- `QaLineup` poses and swaps a far body through CrowdGpu's entry.
