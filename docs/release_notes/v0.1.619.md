v0.1.619: UI Scale in the options, and no more free warping

- The interface size is now a "UI Scale" dropdown in Option > Video, in Windows' steps from 100% to 500%.
- Only the UI scales that fit your window are offered, since the game needs at least an 800 x 600 view of its interface.
- If your window becomes too small for your chosen UI scale, the largest scale that fits is used until the window is large enough again, and the setting says so.
- Option > Video now lists every resolution from 800 x 600 up, whatever your UI scale.
- /uiscale now takes a percentage, for example /uiscale 150, and snaps to the same steps.
- Players can no longer warp themselves to any zone; zone travel in the developer panel is for GM accounts only.
- A refused zone change now says why in the system chat instead of doing nothing.
