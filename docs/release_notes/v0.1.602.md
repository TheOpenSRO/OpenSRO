v0.1.602: player stalls, and fixes for spirits, pets and Gold Time

- Player stalls: choose Stall in the action window, set a title and greeting, list up to 10 items from your bag at your own prices.
- Other players see your booth with the stall name over you; they click to enter, buy and chat in the stall window. Close it with the X.
- A stall closes when you die, teleport or log out, as in the original.
- Stall decorations bought in the mall now show as your booth.
- Angel's Spirit +6 to +10 now gives abilities (inferred from the matching spirits, as this game data has none).
- Event spirits use the server's own spirit tables.
- Taking a spirit off ends the buffs its skills gave.
- A fellow pet whose name has no text no longer shows a raw text key on its plate.
- Gold Time keeps its remaining time correctly across a server restart.
