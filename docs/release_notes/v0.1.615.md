v0.1.615: weapons put away for emotes and stalls, quest marks leave the map

- Action emotes (Hi, Smile, Greeting, Yes, Joy, No) now put your weapon and shield away until the emote ends, as in the original game. Rush keeps them out.
- Other players' weapons and shields disappear during their emotes too.
- A player running a stall no longer holds a weapon or shield; they come back when the stall closes.
- Weapon glows and seal effects disappear and come back together with the weapon.
- Swapping weapons during an emote no longer puts the new weapon in your hands before the emote ends.
- Closing the world map after viewing a quest's area clears that quest's marks, so opening the map normally shows your own area with no quest marks.
- After viewing a quest's area, your first click on the ground brings the world map back to where you are, as in the original game.
- Quest marks no longer appear on a town's own map; they show only on the world map and in dungeons, as in the original game.
