v0.1.616: pets and transports follow you as in the original

- Pets, horses and trade transports following you now stop at walls, buildings, railings and trees, the same way you do, instead of walking through them.
- A following transport heads straight for a spot beside you and gets stuck behind anything in its way, as in the original. Move so its path is clear and it comes after you, so lead it carefully.
- Transports are no longer pulled back to you when they fall behind. Walk back for yours; you still can't go more than 1000 away from it.
- Attack and pick-up pets each take their own spot around you, as in the original.
- Pets keep pace with you: they walk when you walk, run when you run, and hurry to catch up when far behind you while you run.
- A pet left two map regions behind appears on you, as in the original.
- A pet or transport stopped by a wall is drawn standing at the wall, not inside it.
