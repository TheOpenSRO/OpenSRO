v0.1.617: pets' run speed, and other players stop at walls

- Pets now switch between walking and running with their speed, and their run animation speeds up or slows down to match, up to 1.5 times when they hurry to catch up with you, as in the original game.
- Other players, their horses and trade transports, their pets and monsters now stop at walls, trees and railings on your screen, instead of walking into them and jumping back.
