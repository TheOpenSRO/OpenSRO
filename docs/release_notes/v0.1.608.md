v0.1.608: steady quest scrolls, item effects in stalls, and switched models that keep their glow

- The quest scrolls over NPCs no longer flicker between bright and dim; they glow and pulse gently, as in the original. Every effect made of several layers now draws its layers in the same order every frame.
- Items in player stalls now show their effects: seal weapons and armour spin their star in your own stall and in other players' stalls. When you visit a stall that is being changed, the faded item icons keep their bright star, as in the original.
- Pet and transport bags now show the seal star too.
- A summoned pet's scroll now glows around its icon; a dead pet's scroll is tinted blue; reviving a pet or extending its time flashes the scroll once, as in the original.
- An awakened Devil's Spirit now sparkles in your inventory, and once its time runs out its icon is tinted blue.
- Using a gender, avatar or model switcher now flashes the changed item's slot.
- A weapon or shield changed with a Model Switcher now keeps its + glow and its seal effects, for you and for everyone around you, including after you log in again.
- The item mall's character preview and the skin change window's preview now show your switched weapon or shield model with its glow, including while you try on avatars.
- Magic POP now shows every page of your bag, with its page tabs, so you can use Magic POP cards from any page. As in the original, every tab after the first reads "Page 2".
- Put a Magic POP card in the way the original does: click it, then click the card slot (a card from page 2 or 3 can be dropped anywhere in the window). The window shows your gold, locked slots and item effects, marks your chosen prize with a turning edge, plays the original's sounds and animations, and can't be closed while a draw is running.
