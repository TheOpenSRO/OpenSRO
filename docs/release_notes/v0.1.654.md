v0.1.654: status resistance from buffs and items

- Buffs and passives that resist bad statuses now work as in the original: Holy Word, Holy Spell, Fire Shield and the resist passives lower or remove the chance of curses and other statuses landing on you.
- Accessory options now count: Freezing/Frostbite, Electric shock, Burn, Poison and Zombie options shorten those statuses (four 20% accessories leave a fifth of the time); Sleep, Fear, Stun, Disease and Combustion options lower their chance.
- Burn damage over time is now reduced by your magical absorption.
- Darkness lowers your attack rate, Disease makes every other status more likely to land, Powerless lowers the damage you deal, Dissociation raises the damage you take, Panic halves the HP you recover, and Combustion cuts your maximum MP, halves the MP you recover and drains MP, each as in the original.
