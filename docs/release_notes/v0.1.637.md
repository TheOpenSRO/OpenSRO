v0.1.637: the Academy

- Academies: a level-60 character can found an Academy at a Storage-Keeper for 100,000 gold.
- The guardian invites with the Invite button: players below level 40 join as apprentices (up to five), levels 40-59 as assistant guardians (up to two); an invitation waits 30 seconds.
- The Academy window (L) shows the guardian, assistants and apprentices with their levels, grades and online state.
- Guardians can banish, force-graduate and disband; members can leave or graduate. The original waiting times (24 hours after joining, 7 days after founding) and penalties apply.
- The guardian writes the Academy notice; members read it in the window and see its title when they log in or change zone.
- Academy chat with "&" (the &Academy tab), shown once to everyone including the sender.
- Members see each other on the minimap and world map, and get a line when a member logs on, logs off, joins, leaves, is banished or graduates.
- Graduation: an apprentice at 40 (an assistant at 60) graduates through the Guardian Evaluation window; a self-graduating apprentice receives 2,000,000 experience, and the guardian and assistants earn honor points.
- Honor Rank at any Storage-Keeper lists the top academies with their grade, guardian, level, graduates and guild; the daily ranking gives the guardian its rank's buffs.
- The honor store at the Storage-Keepers sells items for gold plus honor points; the tooltip and the buy window show the honor price, and a buy without enough points says so once.
- Guardian Matching (the Matching button): guardians register their Academy, and players browse the board and apply.
- The character window shows your Honor Point.
- The character list knows Academy members, so deleting a guardian's or member's character warns first.
