v0.1.627: horses ride for real, fireworks, guild war marks and long chat lines

- Horses no longer appear as a white blob: ride horses, the unicorn, and trade horses and camels show their real models, for you and everyone around you.
- Fortress hearts, tower-defence towers and event treasure boxes show their real models instead of a placeholder shape.
- Fireworks work: using one sends up its bursts with their bangs, and everyone around sees and hears it.
- Members of a guild at war now wear the guild war mark above their heads.
- Fixed: the server told clients that every guild was at war.
- Berserk orbs earned from a kill fly out of the monster as violet lights, and the gauge lights up as each one lands.
- Your pet's experience from a kill flies to it as small lights.
- Completing or breaking a set of gear plays the set's effect and sound.
- Putting on or taking off a devil's spirit avatar plays its effect.
- Item-use sounds that come after a delay now play at the right moment.
- Every window's close button, the page numbers, the option dots, the quick-party lock and the world map's choice buttons now click.
- Long chat lines, and every other text box, now scroll the way the original does: the end of the text stays 5 pixels in from the right, and moving the cursor left holds it 40 pixels from the edge while the text slides.
- Letters at the edge of a scrolled text box are cut at the box's edge instead of disappearing whole.
- A text box no longer draws its background twice while you type in it.
- The "Silk Charge History" button text is centred exactly as in the original.
