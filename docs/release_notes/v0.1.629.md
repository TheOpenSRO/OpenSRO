v0.1.629: return scroll countdown, ground drops, guild war mark and text cursor

- During a return scroll's countdown you can no longer talk to NPCs, move or equip items, sit, use emotes, open a stall, pick up items or command your pet. Chat and potions still work, and none of these cancels the scroll.
- Using a second return scroll while one is counting down now shows its warning in the middle of the screen as well as in the system log.
- Sealed items (Seal of Star, Moon and Sun) now stand in a column of golden light on the ground, and other drops show their own sparkles.
- Names of items on the ground are now white.
- The guild war mark now shows over every member of a guild at war: crossed blades with a red glow.
- Players standing near a guild's members now see the war mark appear and disappear when a war starts and ends.
- The helper mark, the capture mark and the trade job shields now show their effects.
- The text cursor in every text box (chat, login, names, amounts, guild and stall boxes) is now the original's height instead of a full line tall.
- The cursor in centred boxes like the login ID and password now sits on exactly the same pixel as in the original.
- Fixed a rare case where, after respawning or being moved by the server, a character could stay stuck on the loading screen until disconnected.
