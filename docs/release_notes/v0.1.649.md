v0.1.649: combat numbers at level 101-110, as the original

- Body armour now adds your Strength to physical defence and your Intellect to magic defence, as in the original.
- Monsters' magic attacks are now blocked by magic defence and use their skill's damage percentage; they can no longer critical-hit.
- Monsters use their own attack and parry ratings instead of a level formula.
- Decay and weakness curses now lower your defence, and every curse shows its correct icon.
- Character panel stats and max HP/MP now round down exactly as the original does.
