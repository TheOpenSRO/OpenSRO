v0.1.622: berserk keeps its glow through status effects

- While berserk, your red glow now stays when you are poisoned, burnt, frozen, shocked or stunned, and a status effect ending no longer takes it away, as in the original game.
- When berserk ends, the glow ends with it, even if a status effect is still running, as in the original game.
- Status effect colours on a mounted character start and end normally.
- Skill effects that tint the body now show their colour while they last.
