v0.1.613: the original game's interface everywhere

- Hover help everywhere now appears in the game's own tooltip window, instantly and beside the button, as in the original, instead of a grey system box after a delay.
- Item and skill tooltips flip and stay on screen exactly as the original places them.
- The dead window, the guild name box, the return-point box, and the item-use, buff-cancel, Gold Time and quest give-up questions are now the original game's message boxes.
- The update prompt at start-up uses the game's own message box with Yes/No buttons.
- Text fields work like the original's: a 2-pixel blinking caret in the text's colour, no mouse selection, right-click menu, copy or paste, and a click focuses the field without moving the caret.
- Chat and other game-set limits count bytes like the original, so chat takes 100 Western characters or 50 Korean, Chinese or Japanese ones. The login fields take up to 512 characters.
- Buttons on the login, server list and character select screens use the game's own art in every state, and a disabled button's text greys out as in the original. Buttons no longer keep their hover highlight after being clicked.
- The character select page arrows now appear at their real size, without the extra "<" / ">" drawn on top.
- Character names on the select screen look as in the original: small white text on a dark translucent box.
- The title screen's version line and messages use the game's own small font, centred, with the original's thin shadow.
- The loading screen uses the game's own frame, bar and "Now Loading" art in their original places, with the picture kept at 4:3.
- The composite item window, the stall network page numbers and the Magic POP selection boxes use the original's buttons and combo boxes.
- Party requests, auto potion, item mall and job questions draw their text in the original's style and colours.
- Help shows its pages on the game guide's paper, with the guide's scroll bar and menu.
- The stall chat and the guild GP history scroll with the game's own scroll bar, and the guild notice editor no longer shows a system scroll bar.
- NPC names are white, as in the original.
- The old developer buttons (Log Out, the zone box, Hide UI) and the old debug tabs are gone. Log out with Escape → Restart, and hide the interface with Shift+F10.
- Shift+F1 and Shift+F2 now switch quick slot pages, as in the original. The debug panel (Shift+F3) is only in developer builds; `/copy` still puts a full bug report on your clipboard.
- New chat commands: `/uiscale <0.5–3>` and `/names <0–400>` (both remembered), and `/duel` for this server's duel.
