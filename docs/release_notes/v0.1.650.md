v0.1.650: gear requirements, as the original

- European armour now needs its mastery, as in the original: a robe needs Wizard, Warlock, Bard or Cleric at its degree level, light armour Warrior, Rogue or Cleric, heavy armour Warrior.
- Gear you no longer qualify for stays on but adds nothing (defence, attack, blue options) until you meet its requirement again and log in, change level, or take it off and put it back on.
- Equipping an item you can't use shows the original's warning: "The selected item is unusable because your character's level is too low."
- Robes and garments can no longer be worn together with armour ("Armor and robe cannot be equipped at the same time.").
- GM: /mastery <id> <level> sets a mastery's level (testing tool).
