v0.1.620: status colours end on time, and the pet bar sits behind the portrait

- A status effect's colour (poison green, burn red and the rest) no longer stays on a character after the effect ends.
- A status effect's colour no longer spreads to other characters standing nearby, including your own character when someone next to you is poisoned.
- Berserk and status colours now share one colour as in the original game: the most recent one shows, and when either ends the colour goes.
- The pet's bar under your character portrait now sits behind the portrait ring, race emblem and buttons, as in the original game, instead of covering them.
