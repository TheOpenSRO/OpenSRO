v0.1.623: Help opens every time, and no more see-through gaps in windows

- The Help window opens again after you close it, whether with its X, the H key or Escape.
- Escape now closes the Help window, as in the original game.
- The Help window's side panel has the original's Help, Guide and Quest tabs, and the gap above its list is gone.
- The Help window reopens on its first page.
- The item-use choice window fits its buttons as the original does, with no gap under them.
- The exchange window with portraits no longer shows the world through thin gaps beside the portraits and between its two halves.
- The guild GP history window no longer shows the world along its top edge.
