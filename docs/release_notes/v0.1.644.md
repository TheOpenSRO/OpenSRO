v0.1.644: reset Forgotten World timers

- The Test Items NPC has a new line, "Reset Forgotten World Timers": it clears your Dimension Hole cool time, your Forgotten World re-entry limit and your stored layer at once, so you can open a new layer straight away.
- The Dimension Hole cool time and the re-entry icon disappear the moment you reset, without logging out.
- The Dimension Holes line on the Test Items NPC has a Ticket tab for the Forgotten World entrance ticket, where your game files include it.
- Fixed: a Dimension Hole could still say "Still have time to reuse the item." after logging back in, even when its cool time had been cleared.
