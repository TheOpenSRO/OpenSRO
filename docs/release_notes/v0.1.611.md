v0.1.611: status effects end on time

- Status effects such as poison, burn, darkness, bleeding and confusion now disappear from your character, and from how others see you, as soon as they wear off or you cure them. Before, their effect could hang around for a few seconds, and confusion's music notes for up to 30 seconds.
- A status effect's glow now fades out quickly (in 0.3 seconds) when it ends, as in the original game.
- Buff effects now fade out smoothly when the buff ends, as in the original game.
