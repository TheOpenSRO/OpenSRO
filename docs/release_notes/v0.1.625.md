v0.1.625: potion, pill and kill effects, and text boxes lined up

- Drinking a potion now shows its effect on your character: a red swirl for HP, blue for MP, and its own effect for vigor potions.
- Universal and purification pills now show the green healing halo.
- Return scrolls and every other scroll, including horse and pet summon scrolls, show the scroll's ring when used.
- Pet potions and pet pills show their effect when used; as in the original game, it appears on you.
- Hwan potions and scrolls show their effect and play their sound.
- Nearby players now see your potion, pill and scroll effects, and you see theirs.
- Killing a monster sends glowing experience and skill orbs flying into your character, with a soft chime as each one lands.
- Hits now show what you strike: blood on creatures, stone chips on golems, splinters on wooden foes, ice and metal sparks.
- Plain attacks now show their impact effect, like skills do.
- You now see other players' level-up light when they level near you.
- Pets show the level-up effect when they level, and play the level-up sound when they are yours.
- Calling out a pet plays its summon effect and sound, for you and everyone nearby.
- Buttons make the original click sound, and claiming a quest reward plays the quest sound.
- The chat box's text and blinking cursor sit 5 pixels in from the left and 4 from the top, as in the original, instead of at the very edge.
- Every text box's cursor matches the original: 2 pixels wide, as tall as the text plus 2, blinking every 0.2 seconds.
- Text boxes that the original lays out at the top now draw their text at the top instead of centred.
- Page numbers in storage, guild storage, the shop and party matching are no longer 1 pixel too low, and window titles no longer sit low.
