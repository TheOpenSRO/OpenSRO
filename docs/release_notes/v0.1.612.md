v0.1.612: character select shows your switched gear

- The character select screen now shows a model-switched weapon or shield with its new look, its + glow and its seal effects, just like in the game world.
- Every place that draws your character (the select screen, the create screen, the world, the equipment window, the item mall and skin previews, and the exchange window) now reads from one shared description of your character, so it looks the same everywhere.
- In the exchange window, a trade partner who hasn't fully loaded in yet now shows their + glow and switched models straight away.
- Logging out to character select no longer fills the log with an error every frame.
