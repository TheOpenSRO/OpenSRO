v0.1.618: the trade lizard's walk, and monsters keep up

- A trade lizard ridden at a walk no longer runs on the spot: its model has no walk animation, so it holds its pose while it walks, as in the original game.
- Monsters drawn behind where they really are now speed up to catch up instead of staying behind, and only jump into place when more than 50 units off, as in the original game.
