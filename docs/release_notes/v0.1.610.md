v0.1.610: smoother multiplayer movement

- Small steps now show up on other players' screens exactly where you land, at any speed.
- Your character now walks all the way to the spot you click instead of stopping a little short, even for tiny steps.
- Clicking again near the spot you're already walking to no longer moves you on your own screen without telling anyone else, which used to make you jump on other players' screens when you stopped.
- Tapping or releasing the arrow keys no longer makes you jump on other players' screens: they walk to where you stopped, as in the original.
- Other players who fall behind now catch up smoothly (up to three times their speed) instead of popping forward, as in the original.
- Riders stopping with the arrow keys now stop on everyone else's screen too.
