v0.1.652: the Wrecked Ship runs to its boss

- Wrecked Ship monsters now stand on the ship's deck instead of disappearing under it, so every monster can be found and killed and the dungeon goes on to its boss.
- Closed gates in the Forgotten World now block you until they open.
- Items dropped by monsters on the Wrecked Ship now land on the deck, not in the sea.
- Arriving far along the Wrecked Ship no longer drops you to the sea floor under the deck.
- This client must be used with the matching server update.
