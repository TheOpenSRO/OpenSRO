v0.1.609: guilds complete -- storage, unions, wars, the guild window and manager; readable quests

- Guild storage: talk to the Guild Manager and choose "Use guild storage" (guild level 2 or higher) to share items and gold with your guild. The master and members given the storage right can use it, one member at a time; it holds 30, 60, 90 or 120 items by guild level, and putting an item in costs half the warehouse's fee. Your inventory opens beside it.
- Selecting a member in the guild window now frames their row in yellow, as in the original (the friend list too).
- The guild list sorts by name, level, grade or donated GP, scrolls, and can show grant names. Click the guild notice to read it in full; members allowed to can write it.
- The guild master gives or takes each member's rights (invite, ban, union chat, storage, notice) with "Give authority"; members can check theirs. Members can donate skill points to the guild as GP.
- The Guild Manager now offers the master "Promote the guild." and "Transfer leadership", and lists the GP Usage History of the last 30 days.
- Guild leader vote: when the guild master has been away 45 days, members can start a vote at the Guild Manager; a candidate who reaches 60% becomes the new master.
- Guild Member Summon Scroll: the guild master (guild level 5, in a town) can call every online member to their side, once an hour.
- The guild store's items now cost guild points (GP) as well as gold, shown in the tooltip and the buy window; only the master can buy them.
- Unions: a guild master selects another guild's master and presses Apply in the guild window's Relations > Union tab; they get the original's Agreement box. The Union tab lists the union's guilds (the lead guild in gold); guilds can secede or be expelled, and a union breaks when its lead guild leaves or only one guild is left.
- Union chat (%) reaches every guild in your union; it needs the union chat right (at most 12 members per guild). The lead guild's master can register a union emblem (unionmark.bmp, guild level 5), shown beside the guild emblem.
- Guild war: a guild master declares war from the guild window's Hostility tab with victory points, a period and a gold stake, and the enemy master must accept in person. The enemy guild's names turn red, its members can be attacked outside towns without PK mode, and every kill scores points that both guilds see.
- The guild window's Combat tab shows the enemy, both scores, the stakes and the time left. A war ends when time runs out with one side ahead, when a side reaches the victory points, or a minute after a master requests the end (a surrender); the winning master collects both stakes from the Guild Manager. A guild at war can't be disbanded, and a hostile guild can't be invited into a union.
- Quest details are readable again: orange headings and black text on the parchment, with the original's scroll bar. The reward window's text is black instead of white.
- The map button beside each quest in the quest list opens the world map at the quest's area, with the hunting ground highlighted in gold and the monster's icon; for a quest the map knows nothing about, the system messages say "Does not have any information about that quest."
- Magic POP's bag tabs now read Page 1, Page 2, Page 3.
