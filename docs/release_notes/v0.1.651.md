v0.1.651: no more snapping back while walking

- Fixed being pulled back while running: a run toward a wall (a click on the sky or over the sea, or the Up key) no longer leaves the server's copy of you behind, so you are not teleported back when you reach the wall or click again.
- Your character keeps its pace through a brief freeze (a window opening, a region loading) instead of falling behind where the server has it.
- The server now logs every movement correction with its reason, so a "teleported back" can be traced.
