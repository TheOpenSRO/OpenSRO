v0.1.604: the stall network, moving stall booths, and stall and chat bans

- Stall network: open it from the bottom menu (Menu > Stall network) in a town. Pick a class, type and degree and search; you get 15 results a page, and clicking a column sorts them.
- Select a result to see the seller and whisper them, then Purchase: the item goes straight to your bag without walking to the stall. You can search again every 10 seconds.
- When you open a stall in a town, answer Yes to "Register items with stall network?" to list it on the network. A network sale costs the seller 1% commission (at least 1, at most 100,000 gold), and both sides see who bought what.
- The stall network's menu entry and its question are switched off in the original client; OpenSRO turns them on (an OpenSRO add-on).
- Stall decorations from the mall now play their own animation.
- A jinn pet you have out takes its vendor pose while your stall is open, as in the original.
- A stall's name now sits above your guild line instead of on top of it.
- Stall titles containing the original's banned words are refused with the original's message.
- GMs can chat-ban and trade-ban players (/chatban, /tradeban, /unchatban, /untradeban). A chat-banned player can't chat and sees until when. A trade-banned player can't open or enter stalls or ask to exchange.
- The bottom menu no longer hides its lower rows behind the system message window.
