v0.1.607: guild colours, stall network tooltips, and a clean stall log

- Guild names over players are now orange for everyone in your guild, not only for you, as in the original.
- Guilds in your union now show their name in green, and the colours change at once when someone joins or leaves a guild or a union.
- The server now keeps guild unions. For now a GM forms them with the /union command (/union <guild>, /union leave, /union expel <guild>); players' own union requests are not built yet.
- A stall's name now has its own background box above the player, so the box behind the player's name no longer widens when a stall opens, and renaming the stall resizes its box.
- Stall network: hover a result's item icon to see the item's full description, the same as in your bag (plus, stats, magic options, requirements). As in the original, the durability is shown at its maximum there.
- The stall window's log is now cleared whenever the stall closes, so a new stall (after selling out or closing the old one) starts with an empty log, as in the original.
