v0.1.606: windows that open together, and moving exchange portraits

- Opening your stall, or entering another player's stall, now opens your inventory with it, as in the original.
- The trade center's store now opens your transport's goods window instead of your inventory, as in the original; if that window is already open, opening the store closes it, as the original does.
- Exchanging a coupon with Gori now opens your inventory.
- Escape on an open stall now closes it (or leaves it, as a visitor) the way its X does; before, it only hid the window and the stall stayed open.
- Escape now also closes the pet and transport window, after your other windows.
- The inventory that opens with the Grant window now plays its open sound.
- The exchange window's two character portraits now play their idle stand animation, as in the original, instead of holding a still pose.
