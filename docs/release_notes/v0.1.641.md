v0.1.641: the Forgotten World

- Use a Dimension Hole in a town to open a Gap of Dimensions to the Forgotten World: Togui Village, Flame Mountain and the Wrecked Ship, in level bands from 35 to 110 and up to four grades.
- Only the player who used the hole can see and use its gate; its window shows the world's level band and grade.
- Each world is your own instance: kill every monster to wake Envy, open the blocked doors and draw out the boss, with notices for each step.
- Dungeon Exit crystals take you back to where you used the gate.
- Pillars of Party Member Recall let a party leader summon members, who get an accept-or-decline box.
- After entering, a 5-hour re-entry limit icon (with tooltip and time left) lets you go back into the same world but not open another; it stays after you log back in.
- Worlds last 2 hours and warn at 60, 10 and 5 minutes; when one closes, everyone goes back to their own return point.
- Logging out inside brings you back at your return point, and the gate still leads back in.
- Dimension Pillars can appear while you hunt at the right level, and the Envy they summon can drop Dimension Holes.
- The Wrecked Ship's decks can be walked, and its doors stand where they belong; compound buildings such as Baghdad's houses can be walked on too.
- Fixed: you could not talk to some NPCs after walking in high or low ground areas (Hotan, Samarkand, the Forgotten World).
- Fixed: a Dimensional gate no longer disappears after a teleport within the same town.
- New GM commands: /fgw, /fgwtime, /fgwkill, /fgwpillar and /instance work from the chat console.
- This client must be used with the matching server update (the server and client changed together).
