## v0.1.427 (2026-09-23)

Map objects face the right way; skill hand-off, self heal, cooldown groups

Round 506 -- the headline fix:
  * Every placed map object whose yaw is not 0 or 180 degrees faced the
    wrong way (every Jangan shop, the statues, the portal, most trees).
    The game turns objects by the NEGATED yaw in this client's coordinates,
    read from sro_client.exe's own navmesh loader (MapLoader::FUN_0042b8b0
    -> FUN_0043cda0, a row-vector Y rotation) and measured against the
    navmesh's own data. Buildings and their walkable collision both turn
    the game's way now, so shop doors face the street again.

Rounds 502-505, each grounded in the binaries and adversarially reviewed
(the skill changes take effect on the public server after a refdata
re-export):
  * After an ordinary melee/bow skill the character keeps attacking.
  * Self Heal restores HP/MP; buffs no longer show the HP bar at 0.
  * Skills in the same cooldown group share their reuse timer.
  * Plain attacks use the weapon's base attack skill (a CH sword swing
    hits twice at 60%), so most plain-attack damage goes up.

## v0.1.421 (2026-09-21)

The monster's counterattack lands where the engine says

The counterattack now takes the same centre-biased roll the player's attack
does, with the sides swapped: the monster's attack rate against the parry
summed from worn body armour. Armour therefore does two things now -- it
absorbs damage as before, and it pulls a monster's blow down its range.

Deployments whose item table predates itemdata col 68 keep the uniform roll
exactly as before.

## v0.1.420 (2026-09-21)

Damage lands where the engine says: the centre-biased roll

A blow's position in its weapon's damage range is now the engine's own
centre-biased roll instead of a uniform one -- attack rate against the
defender's parry rate, clamped to [10%, 90%], with a min-of-three excursion.

On real item data this is a modest buff for an armed player: a level-1
character with a Copper Sword sits at a 65% centre where the uniform roll
averaged 50%. Deployments whose item table predates itemdata col 113 keep the
uniform roll exactly as before -- a weapon with no attack rate is missing data,
not a weapon that cannot hit.
