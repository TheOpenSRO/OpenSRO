# Release notes

_Snapshot of the most recent release tags, generated by
client/tools/bundle_docs.sh. A build from a shallow checkout keeps this
committed snapshot rather than an empty file._

## v0.1.485 (2026-10-03)

the original's distance fog replaces the haze around the camera (a temporary Fog row in Video to compare); the original's bloom (its three passes on a 512 target, display values) replaces Godot glow, so the ground draws with more contrast instead of washed out; names and damage numbers are drawn after the bloom; your saved Video settings now apply at start-up (shadows no longer fade as the camera zooms); Circle draws the round shadow under the ten characters nearest you, Detail draws none and casts character shadows on the terrain. High (Detail) shadows: only characters cast real-time shadows now, as in the original -- building and hill shadows are the baked ones. Bloom costs about 0.7 ms a frame at 4K; turn it off in Video if you need the frames.

## v0.1.484 (2026-10-03)

the Option dropdowns' open list as the original draws it (translucent black, centred, its row height); every hover cursor limited to the objects the original holds around you (a teleporter's disc only within reach); one left press is one click (holding does nothing), a far NPC or teleporter is walked to and opened on arrival, a monster's first click selects and the second attacks; the click ring drapes on placed floors like the Jangan plaza; the notice banners drawn as the original's (soft-ended blue band for level-up and uniques, pink-red for GM notices) and an area banner for every named region; a missing sro_client.exe is said on screen

## v0.1.483 (2026-10-03)

the game's own cursors on every screen (gold arrow, attack sword, talk bubble, pick-up glove, blue teleporter disc, hostile-player sword with Alt, stall pouch), found beside or above the pk2 folder and scaled pixel-sharp with the UI scale; the selection ring and click marker as the original draws them (one ring, coloured by what is selected, hidden by a heading move or a despawn); a ground click within 20 of an NPC does nothing, as in the original; the Option dropdown lists in the original's translucent black; the Help window shows Shift+F1-F3

## v0.1.482 (2026-10-03)
