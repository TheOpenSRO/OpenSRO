client/tools/bundle_docs.sh. A build from a shallow checkout keeps this
committed snapshot rather than an empty file._

## v0.1.463 (2026-09-30)

-- reeds fixed (the "Donwhang water"), /speed stutter, Donwhang and Samarkand gates, berserk look, retail icon clicking (rounds 648-652)

- The glitch by the Donwhang ferries was the reeds, not the water. They were drawn as streaks; they now stand as swaying clumps, and the reed fields draw faster (10.7 -> 4.5 ms a frame).
  - Six monsters that showed as grey capsules are drawn as their real models: Thief Boss, Planar, Elemental, Uruchi Dragon, Flame Spirit, Venefica.
- At a raised /speed, a click no longer makes your character stutter back and forth. Other players saw it too; that is fixed.
  - Walking into a wall now stops you instead of running in place.
- Donwhang's and Samarkand's Dimensional Gates can be clicked again.
- Berserk looks like the original:
  - a pulsing red glow and a slight growth;
  - fire on the body, and the start and end sounds;
  - a flash on your own screen.
  - Other players see it, including anyone who arrives or relogs while you're berserk.
- Icons work as in the original. Click once to pick an icon up, and it follows the cursor; click again to put it down. This covers the inventory, equipment, storage, NPC shops, the skill window and the hotbar.
  - Right click uses or casts. A left click on the hotbar now picks the icon up; the number keys still cast.
  - Carry an item to another bag page by clicking the page tab while holding it. The hotbar's page arrows work while holding a skill.
  - Taking an item off puts it in the first free bag slot, as the original does.

Still open:
- Berserk's speed bonus: its values are in a game database table we don't have.
- Alexandria North's gate still can't be clicked (it wasn't clickable before this release either).
- Dropping a bag item on the ground does nothing yet.
