# Release notes

_Snapshot of the most recent release tags, generated by
client/tools/bundle_docs.sh. A build from a shallow checkout keeps this

## v0.1.602 (2026-10-28)

player stalls, and fixes for spirits, pets and Gold Time

- Player stalls: choose Stall in the action window, set a title and greeting, list up to 10 items from your bag at your own prices.
- Other players see your booth with the stall name over you; they click to enter, buy and chat in the stall window. Close it with the X.
- A stall closes when you die, teleport or log out, as in the original.
- Stall decorations bought in the mall now show as your booth.
- Angel's Spirit +6 to +10 now gives abilities (inferred from the matching spirits, as this game data has none).
- Event spirits use the server's own spirit tables.
- Taking a spirit off ends the buffs its skills gave.
- A fellow pet whose name has no text no longer shows a raw text key on its plate.
- Gold Time keeps its remaining time correctly across a server restart.

## v0.1.601 (2026-10-28)

every item in the item mall does what its description says

- Visual scrolls (wings, horns, crowns, capes, trails) and Godbless wings show on your character for everyone, survive relog and death; one look at a time.
- Boots of Speed, speed drugs and damage absorption scrolls work.
- Silk scrolls, Lucky Dice and the lottery tickets (a GM draws with /lottery draw).
- Job, guild, premium and red-stat removers; skill, stat and skill+stat resets; the Skill Leveler (a reset no longer refunds SP the Leveler gave for free).
- Name change at next login, name and title colours, title scrolls and the custom title, the Random Wing Scroll.
- Starter packages deliver into the item chest; Pet Level Up.
- Gender, avatar, devil-spirit, flag, weapon type and model switchers: right-click the tool, then click the item (Escape cancels).
- Silk pets, fellow pets, rides and carts (the Celestial Lizard and Rhinoceros carts now draw); the Clock of Reincarnation and the pet growth potion.
- Devil's and Angel's spirits give their plus-level abilities and skills; the Nasrun extension and magic stone.
- GoldTime PLUS: Gold Time with pause, free return scrolls and reverse scroll uses.
- The repair hammer, storage expansion, arrows and bolts that are spent and reloaded, coloured global chat, the skin change window, Magic POP cards.
- The special reverse scroll: go to a party member or one of your saved locations.
- The EU accessories' damage absorption.

## v0.1.600 (2026-10-27)

avatars in the item mall, try-on, item descriptions and a working search

- The mall search works: type a name and press Enter.
